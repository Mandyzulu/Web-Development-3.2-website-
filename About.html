About Kasi & Suburb Restorations

Welcome to Kasi & Suburb Restorations! We are passionate about breathing new life into forgotten spaces across South Africa. From the bustling streets of Johannesburg to the quieter suburbs of Vereeniging and Pretoria, our goal is to transform abandoned properties into beautiful homes.

What We Do:

  • Buy Abandoned Properties: We offer quick cash deals for neglected, abandoned, or distressed houses.
  • Expert Renovations: Our team restores homes from the ground up, fixing plumbing, roofing, and modernizing interiors.
  • Flexible Sales: Whether you want a fully renovated, move-in-ready home or a "fixer-upper" project to work on yourself, we have options to suit your budget.

Our Mission

We believe that every community deserves safe, beautiful, and well-maintained properties. An abandoned house isn't just an eyesore; it's a missed opportunity for a family to make memories. By buying and restoring these properties, we help uplift local neighborhoods, increase property values, and provide affordable housing solutions for South Africans.

If you have any questions or want to partner with us, don't hesitate to reach out via our contact forms on the home page.
